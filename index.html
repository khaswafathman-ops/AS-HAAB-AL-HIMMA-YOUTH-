Who we are

Faith-led growth. Community impact.

As'haab Al Himma Youth CBO is a community-based youth organisation committed to nurturing morally upright, knowledgeable, skilled and empowered young people through Islamic Da'wah, education and youth development initiatives.

We seek to create opportunities for young people to strengthen their relationship with Allah, acquire beneficial knowledge, develop practical life skills and become responsible contributors to their families and communities.

◎
Our Vision

To nurture a generation of knowledgeable, morally upright, empowered and socially responsible youth who positively transform their communities.

⚑
Our Mission

To inspire and empower young people through authentic Islamic Da'wah, accessible education, mentorship and practical skills development.

✦
Our Core Values

Faith, knowledge, excellence, integrity, empowerment, service, unity and responsibility.

◇
Our Approach

Spiritual, intellectual, personal, practical and social development through participatory programmes.

What we do

Programmes that nurture potential

We bring together sound Islamic understanding, lifelong learning, practical development and meaningful service.

☪

Da'wah & Islamic Development

Salah and Ibadah workshops, Islamic character and etiquette, Qur'an reflection, youth talks, seminars, camps and retreats.

▤

Education & Learning

Academic mentorship, digital literacy, technology awareness, public speaking, communication, creative writing and literacy.

♧

Youth Empowerment

Leadership, entrepreneurship, practical skills, personal development, career guidance, sports and positive recreation.

♡

Community Service

Community outreach, welfare visits, humanitarian assistance, environmental awareness and neighbourhood service.

✧

Mentorship & Leadership

Guidance for spiritual, personal and academic development, helping young people become active contributors.

⌘

Digital & Technical Skills

Building confidence in digital literacy, ICT awareness, creativity, innovation and problem-solving.

Maahad As'haab Al Himma

An Islamic Studies & Technical Institute

A proposed institute envisioned to provide structured Islamic Studies pathways for Mutawassit, Thanawi and, in the future, College-level learners, alongside technical subjects such as ICT and other practical skills.

Our aspiration is to develop a centre where students can grow in faith and knowledge while gaining useful skills that prepare them to contribute positively to society.
